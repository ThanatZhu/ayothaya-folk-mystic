# Illustrated MMORPG quest frame and trial journal

The story frame follows the requested composition: a large scene at the top, followed by a dialogue box with the speaking NPC's existing in-game artwork on the left and their name, dialogue and optional question on the right. The footer keeps previous/next/final-response controls visible. Text scrolls independently on small screens. Story progression still requires the final explicit response; reading, closing or asking a question pays no reward.

Six scene illustrations cover the existing story: dark pier, straw thread, orchard lanterns, moonlit mill, lotus threads and the restored city. Three were generated with built-in image_gen; three reuse existing game illustrations. WebP copies live in assets/story124/ and together are below 3 MB. The four transparent NPC portraits derive from the exact standing frames used by world NPCs, preserving the existing character designs. Generation prompts and reused sources are recorded in assets/story124/prompts.json. Original generated PNGs are retained locally under outputs/story124/originals/.

The teacher trial journal is placed below the actual visible rectangles of the profile, statuses, online button and party. It folds on short screens, has an explicit expand/collapse button, and scrolls long requirements inside the available space. Position is recalculated after layout/buff/viewport changes. A free adjacent position is used if the left stack leaves no room.

Validation: full suite 449 passing before adding the portrait coverage check, followed by targeted narrative/i18n tests. scripts/check-story124.mjs tests real online dialogue gating, loaded scene/NPC images and the top-image/bottom-dialogue/left-portrait structure at four viewport sizes; it reproduces twelve status badges with the trial journal at five desktop/mobile sizes and verifies no overlap with profile, buffs or online status. It also checks all eighteen conversations in English, stat refunds and preserved equipment in a local browser test profile.

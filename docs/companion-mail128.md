# Companions, Gold Bars and player mail

The capture system is retired: no taming/hatching/feeding/return-to-egg commands, capture tutorial, pet bag tab or shop supplies remain active. Old capture items remain sellable keepsakes; old equipped capture pets no longer grant bonuses. The ten premium companions retain their existing ownership, stars, duplicate counts and wallet balances.

The wallet is labelled **ทองแท่ง / Gold Bars**. `balance` remains the same private persisted field, so this is not a wallet reset. No payment processor, paid package, conversion rate or real-money top-up endpoint is enabled. `economy-lab.html` proposes 1 THB = 10 Gold Bars and provides an editable hypothetical revenue/cost calculator. Its assumptions are not production prices or actual analytics. At the existing 100-bar pull price and 3% S rate with an 80-pull pity reset, expected pulls per S are `(1-.97**80)/.03 = 30.4185`; a specified S is uniformly one of three. Eleven copies are needed from zero ownership to 5 stars, yielding approximately THB 10,038 under the proposed conversion. Earnable upgrade materials and selectable exchanges are recommendations, not implemented balance changes.

Admin gift buttons now deliver a private letter rather than changing a bag or wallet immediately. The new **จดหมายและประกาศ** tab sends announcement mail to all realms or one realm, including offline characters. Broadcast announcements currently contain text only; individual character mail can attach cowries, inventory items, Gold Bars and companion copies. No announcement is automatically sent merely by opening the editor. Personal mail has no automatic expiry or deletion. To protect storage it is limited to 200 letters and 350 KB of encoded content; a full mailbox refuses delivery rather than discarding old rewards.

Player mail operations use the existing verified connection. Claims look up server-owned attachments, validate the entire reward, and checkpoint the bag, pet wallet and claimed flag together before acknowledgement. A full bag, stack or cowrie cap rejects the entire claim. Claim markers prevent double rewards even after request receipts rotate. Delivery IDs are idempotent. An uncertain save closes the session and reloads durable state on reconnect; it does not overwrite it with a stale wallet. Gameplay remains live during mail saves, and loot gained meanwhile is queued and then applied. Mail never grants temporary combat invulnerability. Inbox summary counts are cached for simulation snapshots; letter bodies are fetched only when opening the inbox. Mail and premium wallets never import from or export into client-writable character mirrors.

Announcement history is saved beside runtime data, loaded when authority is acquired, and scoped per character's realm. Existing authentication, owner allowlists, origin checks and authoritative session leases protect all admin routes.

Validation: unit/integration tests cover retirement, mixed rewards, full bags/stacks, coin limits, repeated delivery and claim, forged attachments, private mirrors, failed durable saves, concurrent earned loot and persistent/scoped announcements. `scripts/check-pets128.mjs` exercises the real game and admin locally at 1440×900, 844×390 and 390×844; verifies all sixty distinct alpha poses; and checks the economic calculator. `scripts/check-admin127.mjs` also verifies all eight classes, fourteen Master branches and all monster portraits remain operational.

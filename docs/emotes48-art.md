# Emote bubble atlas (0.48) — art brief and drop-in rules

The game reads `assets/emotes48/emotes.png` (placeholder now: numbered speech bubbles). Same file rules as
`docs/items42-art.md`: PNG, transparent, 1536 × 1024, 6 × 4 cells of 256 px, no gaps, **no letters or numbers**.
Each cell is ONE Ragnarok-Online-style emote: a small cream speech bubble (soft outline, tail at bottom-left)
with a bold, cute symbol inside. It pops over a character's head at 44 px, so shapes must read at that size.

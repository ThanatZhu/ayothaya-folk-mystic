# Forge NPC artwork

`blacksmith.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this game. The source is a 1536×1024 transparent RGBA atlas, four columns by two rows, with 384×512 cells. Rendering crops the cells through CSS background positioning; the generated pixels and alpha were preserved. The game's existing `assets/citizens40/masters-arms.png` was supplied as a style reference.

The UI plays three authored hammer contacts at 520, 1050 and 1570 ms and reveals the server result after 2100 ms. Reduced motion keeps the ready pose and the same result gate. The anvil, workpiece and sparks share the actor's coordinate space.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a NEW production NPC animation sprite sheet for Ayothaya Folk Mystic Thai historical fantasy MMORPG. Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its hand-painted chibi pixels, crisp dark silhouettes, warm natural shading, charming mature Thai face and indigo/burgundy clothing. Replace all people in reference with ONE consistent Thai blacksmith NPC, older man with salt-and-pepper topknot and moustache, indigo rolled-sleeve shirt, brown leather apron, burgundy waist sash, bare lower calves and simple sandals. No swords. He uses a short iron forging HAMMER in his right hand, other hand keeps tongs at a small iron anvil on a wooden stump immediately in front of him on the right. 3/4 front view facing right. Strict 4 columns x 2 rows sprite atlas, canvas 1536x1024, each cell384x512. EXACTLY eight frames of the SAME intact full-body person and same anvil, each wholly within its cell, generous transparent gutters. Identical character scale, face, clothing, hammer, tongs and anvil position across frames. Feet and stump bottom align on same baseline within every cell. Each entire pose isolated on REAL TRANSPARENT alpha, no floor, background, shadows, text, numbers, frame borders or floating particles. Full body and hammer visible even when raised. Frame reading order: 1 relaxed ready hammer low; 2 slight windup bent elbow; 3 hammer raised above shoulder; 4 strong hammer strike with head contacting anvil top and body leaning forward; 5 small recoil with hammer just above anvil; 6 second hammer raised high; 7 second contact strike; 8 returned relaxed ready. Strong readable hammer travel, anatomically plausible two hands only, consistent anvil. No flame or effects; game adds sparks. This will be played as an animation; fixed registration matters. Draw every frame as a connected whole-body pose, not separated body parts.
